Our Story

Rooted in
Every Pot

"Good food doesn't need a story — it tells one."

Lagos Bites began in 2012 from a family compound in Surulere. Chef Adaeze Okonkwo grew up watching her grandmother grind melon seeds by hand and slow-cook soups that filled the entire street with their aroma.

Today we serve over 300 guests a day from our Victoria Island kitchen — but every recipe is still made exactly the way it was taught to us. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just real Nigerian food.
